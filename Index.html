📘 Privacy Policy — MCQ Test Zone

Effective Date: November 17, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 17, 2026

Package ID: com.aistudio.mcqtestzone.xqmwkp

Welcome to MCQ Test Zone. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the MCQ Test Zone Android mobile application handles information when you use the app to practice central and state competitive exam quizzes.

1. Information We Collect

MCQ Test Zone does not require account registration, login credentials, or personal profile creation. We do not directly collect your name, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

2. Local Progress & Score Storage

To power your in-app progress tracker (Attempted Tests counter and Recent Attempts history), the app stores quiz attempt records locally on your device using Android's local Room SQLite database and DataStore Preferences.

Your Control: This progress data never leaves your device. You can erase all locally saved attempt counts and test history at any time by opening the Profile/Score tab and tapping Reset Score, or by uninstalling the app.

3. In-App WebView & External Quiz Content

When you select a quiz card, MCQ Test Zone opens the corresponding practice page over HTTPS inside an in-app browser (WebView) from www.mcqtestzone.in. Standard web browser data (such as temporary cache, cookies, or website analytics used by the webpage) may be processed when loading quiz pages.

4. Google AdMob & Advertising ID

MCQ Test Zone uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements so practice tests remain free for students. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and use certain device and diagnostic signals, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate location / IP-derived region and basic device information (OS version, screen size, app version).
  • Ad interaction & diagnostic data (ad impressions, clicks, and performance metrics).

Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at Google's Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

Opt-Out Option: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising anytime via your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads.

5. App Permissions

MCQ Test Zone requests only the minimal permissions required to load online quizzes and serve ads:

  • INTERNET — Required to load MCQ quizzes in the in-app WebView and display ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used to detect network availability and display offline retry prompts gracefully.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ for ad measurement and delivery.

6. Sharing Features

When you tap the Share button, the app opens Android's native share sheet so you can voluntarily share an invitation link with apps you choose. No contact lists or recipient details are read or stored by MCQ Test Zone.

7. Data Security

All network connections from the app to quiz pages enforce HTTPS (usesCleartextTraffic="false"). Local quiz progress remains sandboxed inside your device's private application storage.

8. Children's Privacy

MCQ Test Zone is designed for competitive exam aspirants (SSC, OSSC, OSSSC, RRB, Banking, and B.Ed) and general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a linked webpage, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
